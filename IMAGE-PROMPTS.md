# Additional image prompts

All three assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then converted to 1440 × 960 WebP for this website. No existing photograph was edited. The images are labelled as fictional design concepts in the site.

## public/assets/duplex-editorial.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a believable architectural editorial photograph for a North Alabama renovation company's investor website. A freshly renovated, modest but beautifully detailed one-story side-by-side duplex, clearly TWO separate homes with two distinct front entry doors, white-painted brick, dark bronze window frames, two restrained timber porch canopies, charcoal shingle gable roofs, practical landscaping and a simple shared concrete front path. Mature Alabama deciduous trees and a calm residential neighborhood. This should feel like a real attainable investment-property renovation, not a mansion, resort or fantasy render. Daylight after a light rain, subtle clouds, natural soft shadows, understated warm interior light. Realistic brick mortar, slight irregularity in materials, clean but lived-in context, correct doors/windows/roof geometry. Eye-level professional full-frame camera, 35mm lens, straight vertical lines, 3:2 landscape framing with the entire duplex visible and room around the edges. Avoid cinematic color grading, plastic surfaces, excessive symmetry, fake bokeh, impossible cantilevers, text, logos, watermarks, people, vehicles, wine rooms and bottle displays. The subject is a fictional architectural concept, not a completed Apex project.

## public/assets/deck-editorial.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a true-to-life architectural photograph of a thoughtfully renovated backyard deck on an attainable North Alabama brick home for a premium renovation company website. Warm pressure-treated timber deck boards with believable grain and construction joints, slender black metal railing, three broad well-built stairs connecting the low deck to a simple lawn, black-framed French doors and windows in a muted red-brown brick exterior. An open timber pergola covers part of the deck. Two understated outdoor chairs, one small table, established oak trees beyond. The deck should be attractive, practical, correctly constructed and human-scaled, not a luxury resort. Quiet morning light filtered by nearby trees, subtle shadows and natural colors. Eye-level 35mm architectural editorial photography with straight vertical lines, high material fidelity, realistic slight weather variation. Landscape 3:2 composition with enough context to understand the home and outdoor space. No people, floating structural elements, impossible joints, aggressively cinematic lighting, slick 3D-render textures, text, logos, watermarks or wine displays. This is a fictional renovation design concept, not a completed project.

## public/assets/bath-editorial.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a believable professional interior photograph for a North Alabama whole-home renovation website, showing a beautifully renovated but attainable bathroom. A wide floating walnut double vanity with TWO perfectly aligned undermount basins, thin off-white stone countertop, satin nickel taps, two simple framed rectangular mirrors correctly reflecting the opposite wall, large warm limestone-look porcelain floor tile. On the right, a real walk-in shower behind a clear glass screen with correct hinges, a simple shower head and a recessed niche. Natural daylight from a frosted window, restrained ceiling illumination, linen towel and one modest ceramic vessel. Practical spacious room in a normal house, no hotel extravagance. Crisp realistic grout lines, non-repeating wood grain, natural material variation, no waxy surfaces, no excessive sharpness or teal/orange grading. Shot at eye level from the bathroom doorway with a 28mm full-frame architectural lens, straight vertical lines, 3:2 landscape framing. Accurate plausible geometry and reflections. No people, freestanding tubs, ornate chandeliers, text, logos, watermarks or wine displays. This is fictional architectural concept imagery, not a completed project.
