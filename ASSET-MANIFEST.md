# Apex website asset manifest

## Original identity
Original Apex flame/building mark and wordmark copied without altering geometry from the owner's existing portal brand assets:
- `apex-logo-gold-light.png`: dark header/footer identity.
- `apex-logo-gold.png`: light-surface identity.
- `apex-mark-gold-light.png`: architectural detail.
- Gold favicon and Apple touch icon copied from the existing brand implementation.

## Concept imagery
New AI-generated architectural concepts will be used for the public hero and renovation possibilities. These are not completed Apex projects and will be labeled as concept imagery. No private client images, locations, names, prices, or records will be repurposed.

## Concept files
- `hero-concept.webp`: fictional renovated ranch, hero and renovation introduction.
- `hero-concept-mobile.webp`: responsive crop of the same concept.
- `living-concept.webp`: whole-home renovation possibility.
- `kitchen-concept.webp`: kitchen renovation possibility.
- `bath-concept.webp`: bathroom renovation possibility.
- `investment-concept.webp`: fictional multi-unit investment property possibility.
- `share-card.jpg`: original Apex gold identity plus the fictional hero; generic public brand copy only.

All five originals were generated for this website with GPT Image 2. Their original PNGs are stored outside the public build. Optimized WebP versions retain architectural detail and display concept labels.

## Typography
Manrope Variable (display) and DM Sans Variable (body), self-hosted through the Fontsource packages, with their packaged open font licenses. No external font requests are needed at runtime.
